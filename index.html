How a layout is chosen

Every suggestion follows the same rules, so the result is never arbitrary:

  1. Fit first. A layout is only offered if your room clears its real minimums — e.g. a galley needs ~2.2 m between runs; an island needs ~3.2 m of depth (0.6 m counter + 1.1 m walkway + 0.8 m island + access).
  2. Then your priority. Less walking favours the U-shape; more storage the G-shape; prep & cooking the run with the most worktop; open & social the island or L.
  3. Zones in workflow order. Counters always read store → wash → prep → cook (Consumables, Non-consumables, Cleaning, Preparation, Cooking), so the daily route stays short.
  4. The island holds one zone. The hob when you want to cook facing the room; otherwise the sink, keeping heat on the wall near extraction.
